Senior Quality Engineer

Cover Letter — EPAM

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm writing to express my interest in the Senior Quality Engineer role. With 9 years of experience as an ISTQB Certified Tester, most recently as an SDET at Visa, I've spent that time designing test strategies, driving automation, and keeping delivery quality high across agile teams.

At Visa, I've worked extensively in Java, building performance testing frameworks from scratch (Gatling, then k6), and testing across Docker and Kubernetes environments. Our component test framework is built on Rest Assured, E2E tests are written in Cucumber, and I run API testing through Postman for edge cases and error handling. Earlier at Computershare, I wrote BDD feature files in SpecFlow and contributed to a Selenium WebDriver framework, so BDD and UI automation aren't new ground either.

I'm genuinely excited about this role. The scope, from test strategy through to risk reporting and release readiness, is exactly the kind of ownership I enjoy, and I'd bring that same approach here: clear documentation from the spec stage, close coordination across agile teams, and a focus on quality outcomes rather than just pass or fail counts.

I'd welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background fits this role.

Sincerely,
